# Data

| File | What |
| --- | --- |
| `cases.jsonl` | 109 cases, generated by a language model and reviewed (see `REVIEW.md`): 88 `test`, 21 `fresh` (surface styles used nowhere in `test`, held out for scoring fine-tuned readers) |
| `clauses.jsonl` | The 50 scorable clauses (leaf requirements) of the EU AI Act framework pack (`cdb/eu_ai_act_pack.json`, vendored from Complear) with titles and a link to the official text |
| `raw/` | Generator output before validation |
| `DATASET_BRIEF.md` | The fact definitions and writing rules the cases were generated from, corrected on 17 November 2026 |
| `REVIEW.md` | The 17 November 2026 review: corrections applied and the questions open for the reviewer |

Tiers in `test`: 24 minimal, 19 high-risk via Art. 6(1), 16 limited, 13 high-risk via Annex III,
10 out of scope, 6 prohibited. `fresh`: 6 / 3 / 4 / 5 / 2 / 1 of the same.

## How the cases were made

1. A language model (Claude) wrote descriptions and gold facts from `DATASET_BRIEF.md`, in two
   batches (`raw/cases_a.jsonl`, `raw/cases_b.jsonl`), and a third (`raw/cases_c.jsonl`, 16 cases)
   after the review to cover the Art. 6(3) exception and MDR class I devices, including deliberate traps: non-AI devices
   of high MDR class, AI devices of MDR class I, medical emotion recognition, Annex III systems
   whose exception is cancelled by profiling.
2. `python -m cdb.build_dataset --lint-against <calidus-lab mdr_aiact dir>` validated each case,
   derived its tier and clauses with the rule engine, rejected cases whose intended tier
   disagreed with the engine, whose text named the answer, or that shared an 8-word sequence with
   another case or with the calidus-lab battery (1 rejected).

added after the first review.** The review (`REVIEW.md`) checked every
case and the framework pack against the AI Act, the MDR and EUR-Lex, corrected 42 cases and the

it on a changed case, which then needs review again. `review_sample.csv` is a blank review sheet.
