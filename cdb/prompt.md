You read a short description of a software product and extract facts about it. Answer only with a JSON object holding exactly these keys. Base every value on the text; when the text gives no sign of something, use false or "none".

- ai_system (true/false): the product infers outputs (predictions, recommendations, generated content, decisions) from inputs using machine learning, a language model or another learned method. Fixed hand-written rules, lookup tables and plain calculators are false.
- prohibited_practice (true/false): the product manipulates people or exploits their age, disability or social or economic situation in a way likely to cause significant harm; scores people socially; predicts crime from profiling alone; scrapes facial images indiscriminately; recognises emotions of workers or students for non-medical, non-safety purposes; infers race, political opinion, union membership, religion or philosophical beliefs, sex life or sexual orientation from biometric data; identifies people in real time in public spaces for law enforcement; or generates sexual images of identifiable people without their consent, or child sexual abuse material.
- mdr_class ("none", "I", "IIa", "IIb", "III"): its class as a medical device, stated or clear from its medical purpose and the harm a wrong output could cause. An in vitro diagnostic (it analyses specimens such as blood or tissue) maps its IVDR class A, B, C, D to "I", "IIa", "IIb", "III". Not a medical device: "none".
- annex_iii_area ("none", "biometrics", "critical_infrastructure", "education", "employment", "essential_services", "law_enforcement", "migration", "justice_democracy"): biometrics = remote identification, biometric categorisation or emotion recognition; essential_services includes evaluating or classifying emergency calls, emergency patient triage, evaluating eligibility for public healthcare benefits, and risk assessment or pricing for life or health insurance; employment = recruiting or evaluating candidates, or deciding on, allocating tasks to or evaluating workers. - profiling (true/false): the product automatically evaluates personal aspects of individuals (health, behaviour, reliability, performance) to profile them.
- special_category_data (true/false): health, biometric or other sensitive personal data is processed specifically to detect or correct bias in the model. Training on such data alone is false.
- remote_biometric_id (true/false): the product identifies people at a distance by matching their biometric data against a database. One-to-one identity confirmation is false.
- interacts_with_persons (true/false): people talk or chat with it directly.
- synthetic_content (true/false): it generates synthetic text, images, audio or video, such as a language model's answers or summaries, or generated speech. Verbatim transcription is false.
- emotion_or_biometric_categorisation (true/false): it recognises emotions or sorts people into categories from biometric data.
